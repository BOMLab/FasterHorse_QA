    Quick guide: controls, results and sharing

    Solo: W / up to accelerate, A/D or arrows to steer, S / down to brake and reverse, Left Ctrl to drift, Shift to look behind. On a phone, play sideways; acceleration turns on at GO and the acceleration button toggles it off/on.

    Local multiplayer: join with 2–4 controllers, enter a name beside each horse, click outside the name box and click Play. Right trigger accelerates, left trigger brakes, left stick steers, left face button drifts. Press Start/Menu or Esc for pause. Each player must complete two full laps.

    Icons: circular arrow = retry race; exit door = return to start; speaker = sound; three cards = solo highlights.

    Scores upload automatically. Today and All Time keep your fastest solo time. Multiplayer keeps each completed race, so repeated names are allowed. The same finish must not appear twice.

    Highlights: five local race cards, plus an optional sixth online standings card. Swipe or drag between cards; tap to enlarge. Sharing may open a chooser or save a picture. Choose a private destination or cancel sharing.
