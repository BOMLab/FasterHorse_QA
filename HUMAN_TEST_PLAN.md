# Faster Horse — volunteer playtest

Open [the checklist](index.html). Build date: **28 September 2026**.

The **short checklist is the default**: eight checks for either game mode, or two for the leaderboard website. Choose **Extensive — optional** when starting, or use **Open extensive checklist** while testing, if you want more detail.

Everything is optional in both versions: answers, personal details, notes and screenshots. Skip checks, choose any section, or stop and download a report whenever you want. Failures never block navigation. There are no timed waits, required evidence, video-link fields or instructions to contact a test lead.

## Short checklist

1. Start a race and see whether the track loads.
2. Try the controls and note any problems with pace or response.
3. Check that your name stays with your player.
4. Finish and look at your time and statistics.
5. Find your result on the leaderboard.
6. Start another race.
7. Solo: try Highlights and Share. Multiplayer: check controller ownership.
8. **Any other bugs?** Examples: invisible obstacles, driving on walls, falling through the road, getting stuck or disappearing objects. Choose **No bugs noticed** or **Found a bug**. Details and screenshots are optional.

Website-only checks cover browsing its boards and comparing a result with the game.

## Optional extensive checklist

The extensive catalogue restores 124 checks across both modes and the website. The page filters these to **88 solo**, **83 multiplayer**, or **6 website** checks. They cover:

- Starting, loading, menus, repeated clicks, returning to the start and replaying.
- Driving, drift, boost, collisions, road/lap progression and computer racers.
- Names, unusual input, persistence and correct player assignment.
- Physical controllers, two/three/four-player layouts, disconnects and reconnection.
- Individual results, statistics and finish order.
- Leaderboard sorting, repeat finishes, slower/faster personal bests and scrolling.
- Offline results, retries, reloads and reconnection.
- Highlight cards, sharing, cancellation and repeated races.
- Touch controls, rotation, fullscreen, screen sizes and backgrounding.
- Website boards, pagination and matching results.

Core game checks are a useful starting point, not a gate. Equipment, completed races or existing board entries mentioned under a check describe when it is useful; skip it if those conditions do not fit. Midnight cases are only for someone who happens to play across UK midnight. No need to schedule a session or wait for a particular time.

## Navigation and reports

In the short version, use **Previous / Next** or **Skip**. In the extensive version, **Section** chooses a broad area and **Subsection** chooses a feature group; Previous / Next still moves between checks. **Mode** shows the selected game mode. Extensive checks also show brief context where needed.

Choose **Works**, **Problem**, **Can't test**, or **Doesn't apply**. Leaving a check unanswered is fine. The other-bugs question uses **No bugs noticed / Found a bug** instead.

Short and extensive answers are saved separately, including when their test IDs overlap. Switching versions does not lose progress or transfer a pass to different instructions.

**Download report** exports the currently selected checklist only, at any point—even with no answers. Its button stays visible at the bottom as you scroll. A large download panel appears once every check has an answer; it does not imply every test passed. Use **Preview** to see the summary without downloading. Unanswered checks remain NOT TESTED. Partial reports never claim that every check passed. Downloads happen only on a click.

The HTML report embeds attached screenshots and opens in a browser. **Open previous report** restores its checklist version, answers, notes and screenshots after confirmation. Import replaces the currently saved session, including the other checklist's saved answers; download both reports first if you want to keep both. Original report files remain unchanged. Reports from older versions are supported; checks whose instructions changed return to NOT TESTED while retaining notes and evidence.

The page saves locally and makes no leaderboard API requests. The actual game still uses its online services when you race or open its leaderboards.
